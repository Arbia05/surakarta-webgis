Surakarta

Surakarta, often known as Solo, is a historically and culturally rich city in Central Java, Indonesia. As the heir to the Mataram Kingdom, it boasts significant heritage sites, traditional arts, and vibrant markets.

This WebGIS platform provides an overview of the city's essential infrastructure. Explore the interactive map to understand the spatial distribution of these critical assets within the city boundaries.
